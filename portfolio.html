My name is Shawn Harrell, but you can call me Hero. I am an artist and educator based in St. Louis, MO. For most of my life I have found ways to express myself artistically through drawing, painting, collaging, and graphic and fashion design; often times combining these skills. Since 2020 I have been an education consultant through my company Sovereign University. During this time I led a full-day, 9-month experiential learning micro-school and cooperative, focused on exposing youth to opportunities in agriculture and technology. Additionally, during this period I have developed curriculum for local organizations such as ⚽ St. Louis CITY SC, 🏡 Invest STL and 🌽 Phi Global Farms. I have also partnered with many other independent St. Louis organizations such as Cohoma Orchirds, ACRES farm, Teens of Tomorrow and Legacy Institute to bring to life educational experiences. Before leading Sovereign University, I worked in Higher Education as an administrator, instructor, and advisor. As I now look forward, I am drawing from my breath of knowledge and previous experiences to create meaningful digital learning experiences. As an aspiring software engineer, my goal is to reach more families through the development and deployment of immersive, culturally-competant, interactive web applications and sites.



Organization Outcome Year
St. Louis CITY SC In collaboration St. LouisCITY SC and Atlas Public School, I developed a curriculum
for a health and fitness camp that ran from Monday, June 26, 2023 - Friday, June 30,
2023, from the hours of 9am - 3 pm at The LegacyCenter, 6850 Normandale Drive St. Louis,
MO 63121. This education experience was designed to provide youth athletes with physical
and mental wellness training to encourage success on and off the field. During the week,
athletes participated in activities to support their physical fitness, soccer technique,
mindfulness, nutrition, and wellness.		 2023
Invest STL In collaboration with another educator I designed a 9-month cohort-based curriculum
system with structured modules, session plans, and facilitation guides. We also developed
frameworks integrating storytelling, historical analysis, and place-based learning. To ensure
success we evaluated and refined curriculum components to improve clarity, usability, and
participant engagement. It was also necessary to collaborate with stakeholders to align
curriculum outputs with program goals and delivery models. For sustainability we built
adaptable curriculum structures designed to evolve based on participant feedback and program
needs		 2026
Legacy Institute I developed curriculum and taught the course, "A History of Black Resistance"
The purpose of the course was to present Melanated (Black) history from the perspective of
successes opposed to failures, and victories opposed to trauma. This course intentionally
presented Melanated history from a global perspective and guided learners though resistance
measures throughout the African diaspora. Based on participant surveys learners completed the
course with a better understanding of the role resistance has played in the global history of
Melanated people. Learners were challenged with level-specific reading and research assignments,
participated in reenactments, and completed summaries and reports to demonstrate competancy.
To be most inclusive, readings relied heavily on open-source materials, and learners were
encouraged and challenged to critically think about how the history under exploration effects
their current reality and place in world history.
